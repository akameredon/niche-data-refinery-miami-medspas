## Gaps Observed
1. **Pricing transparency**: Almost no clinic publishes a full public price list. Opportunity for an agency to create “transparent pricing” as a differentiator.
2. **Review theme depth**: Most public commentary focuses on results and staff friendliness.
3. **Hiring visibility**: Only a minority of clinics publicly post injector roles. Those that do signal growth.
4. **Wellness adjacency**: IV therapy / NAD+ / peptide clinics are growing fast alongside traditional med spas.
5. **Physician-led consistency story**: Strong trust signal that pure volume clinics cannot easily copy.

## Recommended Agency Plays
- For a new or mid-tier client: Own a neighborhood rather than city-wide.
- For premium clients: Lean into credentials + before/after consistency + low complication narrative.
- Watch multi-location brands for acquisition or partnership opportunities.
