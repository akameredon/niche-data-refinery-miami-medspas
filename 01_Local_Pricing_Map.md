## Key Observations
1. **Geography premium exists**: Brickell, Coral Gables, South Beach / Bal Harbour command higher rates than Doral, Kendall, or inland Southwest.
2. **Transparency is low**: Exact current prices rarely listed publicly; most require consultation. Intro specials and membership pricing are common acquisition tools.
3. **Physician vs nurse injector**: Physician-led practices often sit at the top of the range and market consistency/safety.
4. **Competitive pressure**: Multi-location brands and high-review clinics use specials to fill books while maintaining perceived quality.

## Actionable for Agencies
- Clients in value segments can be positioned against $12–14 clinics.
- Premium positioning should emphasize injector credentials + review volume rather than pure price.
- Monitor intro specials weekly — they signal capacity or acquisition aggression.
